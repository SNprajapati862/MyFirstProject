Earth

  • Earth is the third planet from the Sun.

  • the only known planet in the universe that supports life.

  • It has the right conditions — air, water, temperature, and gravity — to sustain millions of species, including humans.

  • About 71% of Earth’s surface is covered by water, mainly in oceans, seas, rivers, and lakes.

  • The remaining 29% is land, made up of continents and islands.

  • Earth is about 12,742 km (7,918 miles) in diameter.

  • About 8 billion people live on Earth.

  • Earth’s gravity keeps the atmosphere and everything on its surface in place.

  • The planet’s magnetic field protects us from solar winds.
