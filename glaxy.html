Glaxy

  • A galaxy is a vast collection of stars, planets, gas, dust, and dark matter all held together by gravity.

  • Galaxies are the building blocks of the universe, containing billions or even trillions of stars.

  • Our Solar System is part of a galaxy called the Milky Way.

  • Our home galaxy —a spiral galaxy about 100,000 light-years across.

  • Contains over 100 billion stars, including our Sun.

  • At its center is a supermassive black hole named Sagittarius A*.

  • There are over 2 trillion galaxies in the observable universe.

  • Galaxies often form clusters and superclusters, linked together by gravity.

  • The light from distant galaxies takes millions or billions of years to reach us — when we see them, we’re looking back in time!
