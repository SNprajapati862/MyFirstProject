🪐 Solar System ☄️

  • Our solar system consists of the Sun and all the objects gravitationally bound to it, including eight planets, dwarf planets, moons, asteroids, and comets.

  • The eight planets are Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

  • The Solar System is about 4.6 billion years old.

  • The solar system formed by collapsing cloud of gas and dust called a solar nebula.

  • Everything in the Solar System orbits around the Sun due to its powerful gravity.

  • The edge of the Solar System extends far beyond Neptune into the Kuiper Belt and the Oort Cloud.
