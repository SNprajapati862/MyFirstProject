Sun

  • The Sun makes up over 99.8% of the total mass of the Solar System.

  • It provides the light and heat necessary for life on Earth.

  • It is a G-type main-sequence star, also known as a yellow dwarf.

  • It is about 93 million miles (150 million kilometers) from Earth.

  • It’s a huge ball of hot gases, mainly hydrogen and helium, undergoing constant nuclear fusion.

  • Light from the Sun takes about 8 minutes and 20 seconds to reach Earth.

  • Its gravity holds the system together and provides heat and light.

  • it is the largest object and contains over 99% of the solar system's mass.
